About me

I grew up in Bulgaria, went to the Sofia High School of Mathematics and moved to Leeuwarden in 2022 to study Creative Business at NHL Stenden. Across three years of concept projects I've worked on everything from campaign plans to full productions, so I'm comfortable taking an idea through planning and the shoot to a finished edit.

I've always loved music. I started playing guitar as a kid, and then came DJing, and with it remixes, mashups and tracks that are now past 500,000 streams on Spotify, 100,000 listens a year on SoundCloud and 1.5 million views on YouTube. I've performed and MC'd at clubs, bars, private events and dinner parties.

From 2023 to 2025 I co-founded and led the PR team of the Bulgarian Society Leeuwarden. I shaped the society's look and grew its Instagram from nothing, sat in on interviews to pick new members, joined the weekly team meetings, and handled the club owners and venues for our events, where I was also the DJ.

I did a six-month minor in Intercultural and International Communication and a semester exchange in Málaga, so working with people from everywhere is normal for me.
