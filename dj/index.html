Zander Production

DJ, MC and the guy filming it

I started playing clubs and events in 2020 and it turned into a real job, so I registered it with the KVK. I do all of it: bookings and client contact, the rider, the set, playing and MC-ing, and then filming and editing the recap and promo content.

Playing for a crowd every weekend teaches you to read a room and change plans on the spot. That's the same thing I do in an edit. Remixes, mashups and my own tracks are past 500,000 streams on Spotify and 1.5 million views on YouTube.
